Our Philosophy

Most productivity tools are overcomplicated. ToDoList removes the friction — no endless menus, no intrusive pop-ups. You type your task, and the app takes care of organizing it for you.

It's built for people who want a clean workspace without sacrificing powerful features.

How It Works

⚡

Quick Entry

Type your task, add #Section to categorize, and @Name to assign. Press Enter — done.

👥

Team Collaboration

Share your lists with teammates. Assign tasks individually or as a group. Everyone stays in sync.

🎨

Make It Yours

Custom wallpapers, accent colors, dark or light theme — your workspace adapts to you.

🌐

4 Languages

Switch between English, French, Chinese, and Russian at any time — no page reload required.
